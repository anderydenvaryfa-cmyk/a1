Couture Protocol

The Four-Stage Commission Fitting Process

Four meticulous appointments ensure complete anatomical harmony and flawless architectural draping.

STAGE 01

Silhouette Consultation

Private drape analysis, color swatching under warm evening lighting, and anatomical measurement mapping.

STAGE 02

The Cotton Toile Fitting

Sculpting a preliminary muslin mock-up to calibrate posture, corset tension, and waistline proportions.

STAGE 03

The Silk Draping

Cutting and hand-basting pure silk taffeta panels, pleating volume, and aligning iridescent grain lines.

STAGE 04

Final Salon Reveal

Final hemline calibration in gala footwear, steam relaxation, and certified packaging in archival garment boxes.
